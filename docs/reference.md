# Worldbuilding Wiki: Consolidated Reference

Oct 2, 2026 · @Be

## Overview

The world rests on three forces. Order and Chaos govern the universe as equals; the Void is older than both and is a threat to them.

- **Order** governs how existence is *lived*: identity, connection, meaning. It acts through **Life** (Body, Mind, Soul) and **Ether**.
- **Chaos** governs how the physical universe *functions*: space, time, gravity, energy, matter. It acts through four **Domains** and the **Primordials**.
- **Void** is outside both. It is indifferent, not malicious, but not bound by any law, so its intrusions (**Anomalies**) can attack, threaten, and destroy.

The only ranking in the world is **Primordial over Anomaly**. It is not Chaos over Void.

## Wiki structure

The wiki has six sections: Places, Powers, People, Bestiary, Objects and Lore.

| Section | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Places | World map, nations, regions, states, avenues; layered places such as Sek'luz above Mughierel |
| Powers | Order, Chaos and Void trees; Aspects, elements, Domains, poles, Interferences; Ether techniques, Awakening, Transcendence; Void Pact and Chaos Contract |
| People | Characters and groups (teams, the Guild, the cult), with conditions such as Hybrid or Etherless shown on the page |
| Bestiary | Incomplete Beings, Anomalies, Primordials, Abyssals |
| Lore | History (such as Zhoter's founders), Institutions (Hunters, Lupins), Concepts (Life, eye colors, canon status), Events and secrets (the Mughierel experiments, the Gardener Project), Legends |
| Objects | Crystals; Cursed Objects (including cursed weapons and the artifact holding the Gardener's Soul); Remnants; Potions (stub, alchemy in progress); Equipment such as Ether-forged weapons and Zayn's greatsword; Technology such as Flow Posts, vacuum towers and steam locomotives. Symbols like a cape or a blindfold stay a section on the character page unless one becomes important. |

**Between sections.** An entry has one home page, and the other section holds a redirect to it. Abyssals live in the Bestiary and are reached from People; crystals and cursed objects live in Objects, with redirects from the element pages in Powers and from Incomplete Beings in the Bestiary. The Guild lives in People and also shows on the Trovic page. Hunters and Lupins are an institution in Lore, while their teams are in People.

## Entry types and shared shape

Every entry has the same shape: a few **fields** (filterable), **links** to other entries, and **sections** (long text). Entries live inside a **project** (a world or story). Rule of thumb: filter or sort by it → field; has its own page → linked entry; a story told in sentences → section.

| Type | Examples | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Character | Aria Vale | Fields, links, sections (see People and groups) |
| Group | Hunter team, cult, Guild of Trovic | One type, with a *kind* field |
| Place | Trovic, countries, regions | Optional parent place; Chaos creatures link to a habitat |
| Power | Order, Bending, Fire; Chaos, Dimension, Space | Tree: category, system, specific, each with one parent |
| Creature | Spectre, Anomaly, Primordial | Exactly one parent system |
| Crystal | Fire Crystal, Pure Ether Crystal | Eleven entries, passive effects per crystal |
| Item | Cursed Object, Remnants | Cursed weapons are a section on Cursed Object |
| Lore page | Hunters, Lupins, Life, Eyes | Explanation only, no fields |

Shared on every entry: **aliases** (search finds in-world names), **visibility** (see In-world knowledge), and **links** with optional notes.

**Canon status** on every entry and section: *canon*, *legend* (an in-world belief that is not true, such as the Ether rain of the south) or *retired* (obsolete notes and names, kept hidden as aliases). Old and current concepts never mix on one page.

**Link kinds:** member of (role, current or former, note) · lives in / hometown · relative of (kind) · hunts · holds pact or contract with · balanced by · parent of · becomes (Cursed Object to Liminal).

## Order

Order has two pillars, **Life** and **Ether**. Ether is the real power system; bending is only one part of it. Ether professions include Etherurgists, Crystal Engineers, Artificers, Chargers and Alchemists, who study Ether and its applications, above all potion making (water, Ether and ingredients, each ingredient adding an effect; details still in progress).

- **Life** (lore page, tied to the bestiary): Body, Mind, Soul. The Soul bridges Life and Ether.
- **Ether**
  - **Five Aspects:** Balance, Harmony, Cycle, Emotion, Growth. Each has two elements.
  - **Bending** (manipulating elements): ten elements, one fixed per character, with a flat color. Every living being can have an element, animals included (details in progress).
    - Balance: Light (white), Darkness (black)
    - Harmony: Earth (brown), Air (purple)
    - Cycle: Ice (cyan), Lava (orange)
    - Emotion: Fire (red), Water (blue)
    - Growth: Plant (green), Electricity (yellow)
    - Non-Bender (gray): no element; born when both parents hold opposite poles of one Aspect
  - **Techniques:** Manipulation, Creation (needs a crystal), Ether Perception (Presence, Essence), Ether Suppression and Expansion, Aura Suppression, Reinforcement, Infusion, **Transformation** (the Ether Body, in the element's color)
  - **Awakening:** the Soul aligns with one Aspect (independent of element). No crystal needed for creation, plus Aspect-specific abilities per person. A Non-Bender who awakens gains an element.
  - **Transcendence:** all five Aspects integrated. One permanent change (even the element), direct Ether manipulation, **Ether Awareness**, Echoes of the Past.
- **Ether Crystals:** eleven entries (one per element plus Pure Ether), each with its own passive effects, mostly everyday life. Linked to an element only. Non-Benders carry none.
- **Incomplete Beings** (Order's bestiary, each missing one foundation):
  - **Spectre**: no Body. Resolved by being *released*.
  - **Cursed Object**: no Mind. A separate entry; may become a Liminal.
  - **Liminal**: no Mind. Resolved by being *untangled* (remove the anchor, then resolve the Soul).
  - **Doppelganger**: no Soul. Resolved by being *confronted* by the original.

Creature pages share three fields: **Composition**, **Absent foundation**, **How it is resolved**.

## Void

The Void intrudes on reality and exposes its cracks. Reality's Ether pushes back, so most intrusions collapse at once. The ones that survive are **Anomalies**.

- **Four Interferences** (the four Void creature types), each a rupture of one pillar:
  - **Negation** (Ether): things become inconsistent, causality stops working
  - **Mutation** (Body): boundaries dissolve, forms fuse and collapse
  - **Sensation** (Mind): several incompatible realities experienced at once
  - **Manifestation** (Soul): manifesting what was not there: imitations of identity with no one home, or a whole new place, such as the Arena of Isolation
- **Anomalies:** individual entries, each under exactly one Interference. Body and Mind, but no Soul and no Ether. No two alike, so **eye color varies freely**. Temporary: they vanish and leave **Remnants**.
- **Void Pact:** an Anomaly borrows a willing vessel's Soul and Ether. The vessel gains the Anomaly's distortions, personal to each pair.
- **Hybrid:** a vessel who keeps Body, Mind, Soul and Ether. The Anomaly is a permanent second presence and must be fed Remnants. Psychological bleed is normal. Every Pact holder is a Hybrid and hunger is the default. How often they feed and how the Anomaly relates to the vessel varies, for example a silent passenger the vessel barely interacts with.
- **Abyssal:** a failed Hybrid. The Anomaly becomes the vessel. Irreversible, reshapes its surroundings into Dead or Distortion Zones.
- **Remnants:** the resource that feeds Anomalies and Hybrids. The cult's "summoning" is really reconstruction: Remnant + vessel + ritual rebuilds a destroyed Anomaly. Most are unstable, and many Anomalies the Guild fights in Zhoter are failed or abandoned reconstructions. The Gardener is a one-of-a-kind legend: an Anomaly that underwent Transcendence and connected to Order, gaining Ether and a Soul. Its Soul is bound in a Cursed Object hidden in Zhoter. It was originally a Manifestation Anomaly; it now has the status "transcended" and naturally holds both Void and Order powers. The Herald's plan to make a pact with it probably cannot work, because it is no longer a Void entity (the story will explain).
- **Breaking a Pact:** only by starvation, which ends in freedom or an Abyssal. The one other exit is a Chaos Contract (see Rules).

A Pact blocks **Transformation**, because Transformation depends on the identity of the Ether and a Pact compromises it.

## Chaos

Chaos is one force that mortals divide into four **Domains**, each with two **poles**. A character holds exactly one pole.

| Domain | Pole 1 | Pole 2 | Primordial with Contracts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dimension | Space | Time | Yes |
| Attraction | Gravity | Dark Energy | None yet |
| Flow | Energy | Entropy | Yes |
| Matter | Substance | Antimatter | None yet |

**Primordials**

- Exactly four exist, one per Domain. Each is a Domain given living form: conscious and primal, closer to a kaiju than a god, reasoning in alien ways.
- No Ether and no Soul. Held together only by its Domain. **Eyes are full black.**
- Without a Contract it is in *maintenance mode* and must keep consuming Ether. With Contracts it lives more freely.
- If destroyed, it returns to Chaos and is reborn after millions of years as a **new individual**. Each individual gets its own page, with status and predecessor/successor links.
- Lending both poles weakens it, but it is still kaiju-scale and very hard to kill.
- Unlike an Anomaly, a Primordial borrows only Ether, never Soul.

**Chaos Contract** (everyday word: pact)

- Willing on both sides. The host's Ether anchors the Primordial; the host gets a fragment of one pole.
- A Primordial holds at most two Contracts, one per pole, and loses each pole it lends. The host never chooses the pole.
- The host loses their element (bending only) and has permanently reduced Ether reserves, but keeps the other Ether abilities.
- The Primordial cannot control the host, but wants it alive.
- **Side effect, set per pole:** emotions simplify when the host is under emotional strain or uses the powers, at varying intensity. Energy: manic, joyful, ecstatic. Entropy: closing down, feeling nothing. The other six are to be defined.
- It fades if the host stops using the powers, or more reliably if the other holder of the same Primordial balances them: passively by being near, actively by using their own power. Contract holders can use Transformation, but with difficulty; with the other host of the same Primordial it comes easily.

**Etherless:** when a Primordial is destroyed, its holder loses the power and all their Ether. They stay human, with black eyes and no Ether abilities. Has never happened in the world, and nobody knows it is possible.

**Known hosts (Flow).** Sokah (birth name Samuel, author-only) holds Energy, in gold. Yel (birth name Isabel, author-only) holds Entropy, in silver. The Mughierel experiment documents showed that a human can coexist with an entity that bears Ether. In those documents they appear only under the aliases "Chaos" (Sokah) and "Eli" (Yel). The cult studies the documents, not the two of them, and does not know who they are. The Flow Primordial is not named yet. As the two hosts of one Primordial, they are each other's balancing link. Dimension's hosts (Space, Time) are not named yet.

## People and groups

**Character template**

The infobox keeps the four groups of the full profile:

| Group | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| Header | Name, Title (optional), Caption (team), Subtitle, Image |
| Personal | Age (text), Birthday, Eye color (derived), Gender, Hair color, Height, Hobby, Phobia |
| Origin | Hometown (place link), Raised in (place link, optional) |
| Relationships | One line per relation kind, for example Partner, Rivals (links to characters) |
| Combat | Bending (element link), Class, Member of (group link), Allegiance (optional note), Weapon |

**Derived, not stored:** Region (from the place tree), eye color (from the element or condition), crystal, pole side effect, condition (Hybrid, Abyssal, Etherless), Transformation allowed.

**Page sections**, in order: intro under the name, Personality, Backstory, Character Flaws (with an optional short name, such as "Rigorous"), Abilities, Relationships (one subsection per person), Trivia (facts about the character's creation, not the world). Backstory and the other sections link to places, characters and groups. A holder of a Pact or Contract gets a power section named after the system (for example, "Void Power – Manifestation"): the Interference or pole, the Anomaly or Primordial partner, and the power itself, without repeating general rules like hunger. Other custom sections can be added.

**Relations** are links with one or more kinds (partner, former partner, rival, friend, mentor, found family) and a short note written from that character's side. Each relation also appears on the other character's page. One pair can have two kinds, for example rival and close friend.

Only Name is required, and "Unknown" is a valid value: Dadá's page is deliberately empty as a running joke. A character can hold two powers from different categories, but loses something (see Rules). Element is fixed; the exceptions are listed in Rules.

**Groups** are one entry type with a *kind* (hunter team, cult, guild, NPC group) and two separate settings:

- **Legal status:** registered hunter team or not
- **Alignment:** hero, villain, or other

They are independent, so a heroic illegal team (a "good Lupin") is possible.

- **Hunters:** a government public-service profession, like firefighters. No central organization; each team acts on its own. Hunters can kill monsters, never humans. A lore page.
- **Lupins:** illegal monster hunters, working solo, duo or trio. A lore page, plus a label on teams. A team can have aliases and a legal-status history: Susie's team was illegal under the alias Team Lupin, and after becoming legal it is Team Kai (Kai, Leon, Susie).
- **Teams:** every legal hunter must be on a registered team of one to three members.
- **Guild of Trovic:** a community guild in Trovic, the main setting of the story. It is a middleman: it prepares missions, files the reports, and the government pays it to pay the hunters. Joining is optional and team-level, and only Trovic has one. Staff: two administrators and two retired hunters who founded it, Magnus and his wife Bertha.
- **The cult:** the Children of the Abyss, a Void-aligned, unregistered, villain group of 13: the Herald of the Void plus the G12, in four divisions (Containment and Preservation, Study and Reconstruction, Execution and Ritual, Philosophy and Strategy). Almost all hold Void Pacts. Three do not: the Herald, one who only pretends, and one waiting for the right Anomaly. The stated goal is coexistence with Anomalies; the hidden goal is the Gardener Project. Lara, Kate and Lydia will defect, become a registered hunter team in the Guild, and, as Hybrids, try to get rid of their Pacts; each will have two memberships (the cult as former, the new team as current). Groups can nest, so the G12 and its divisions are sub-groups with roles such as High Ritualist.

**People section plan**

- **Pages:** People home, character list, character page, group list, group page. Create and edit forms come later.
- **Character list:** grouped by team by default and sorted alphabetically. Other options: group by element, region or nothing; sort by name, age or recently updated. The choice lives in the URL (`?group=team&sort=name`), so a view can be shared and survives a refresh.
- **Which group counts:** a character appears under the top-level group of their *current* membership. Former memberships show only on their page or in a filter. Planned memberships (such as Lara, Kate and Lydia joining Team Kai) never show in the public view. Characters without a team go under "Unaffiliated" at the end.
- **Group order:** a manual `order` field set by hand, meaning order of appearance in the story. Ties and missing values fall back to alphabetical order, with groups lacking a number last.
- **Sub-groups:** members are listed under the top group (for example Children of the Abyss). The division (Containment, Study, Execution, Philosophy) shows as a label on the character page and the group page, and each division keeps its own page with its members.
- **Memberships** are records with a role and a status (current, former, planned). **Relations** are two-way, with a note from each side. A relation can point to someone with no page yet, shown as plain text until the page exists.
- **Build order:** character page from a hardcoded object with `InfoBox` and `WikiSection` components; Zayn moved to a seed file and read through one function; character list from several seed files; relations; group page with memberships; derived values; filters and search; forms.

## Rules the wiki can check

**Power combinations**

| Situation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Order + Void | Allowed. Transformation is lost while the Pact lasts. |
| Order + Chaos | Allowed. The element is lost (bending only) and the other Ether abilities stay; Ether reserves are lowered. |
| Hybrid takes a Chaos Contract | The Anomaly vanishes with no Remnants, the Pact ends, no starvation test, no Abyssal risk, Transformation returns. |
| Abyssal seeks a Chaos Contract | Rejected by the Primordial. |
| Chaos Contract, then Void Pact | Technically possible, does not work. |

**Element changes.** The element is fixed, except:

| Event | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Transcendence | Can change it |
| Awakening (Non-Bender only) | Creates one |
| Chaos Contract | Removes it (bending only) |

The character page keeps an *original element* and a note on how it changed. Both Awakening and Transcendence are extremely rare.

**Other checks**

- A Pact (Void) blocks only Transformation, not Reinforcement, Infusion, Perception or bending. Non-Benders have every Ether ability except bending, so Transformation is theirs too, in gray.
- Each Anomaly has exactly one Interference; each creature has exactly one system.
- A Primordial has at most two holders, and the two must hold different poles. Balancing links only connect the two holders of one Primordial.
- A Contract status is *active*, *ended by the host's death* (pole returns to the Primordial) or *ended by the Primordial's destruction* (host becomes Etherless).
- A legal hunter must be on a registered team; a team has one to three members.
- Hunters kill only monsters, never humans.
- A Non-Bender or an Etherless character shows no crystal.
- Pacts exist only in Void and Chaos, never for Order creatures.

## Eyes and colors

Eye color follows from what a being has or lacks. Transformation uses the same color as the eyes.

| State | Eyes |
| --- | --- |
| Soul missing (Doppelganger) | Pure white, no iris or pupil |
| Ether missing (Etherless, Primordial) | Pure black, an absence (Anomalies also lack Ether but vary freely) |
| Order element | Flat element color |
| Non-Bender | Gray |
| Chaos Contract holder | Gem or metal color of the pole, metallic and faintly glowing |
| Anomaly | Varies, any color |

**Chaos pole colors**

| Domain | Pole | Theme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimension | Space | Emerald |
| Dimension | Time | Sapphire |
| Attraction | Gravity | Diamond |
| Attraction | Dark Energy | Onyx |
| Flow | Energy | Gold |
| Flow | Entropy | Silver |
| Matter | Substance | Ruby |
| Matter | Antimatter | Amethyst |

Several colors overlap on purpose (emerald and Plant, ruby and Fire, diamond and Light, onyx and Darkness, gold and Electricity). **Finish** tells them apart: Order colors are flat, Chaos colors are metallic and glow. Etherless and Primordial black is a total absence, unlike Darkness or onyx. Each of these pages carries a short "similar to" note.

## Void Pact vs Chaos Contract

|  | Void Pact | Chaos Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partner | Anomaly (no Soul, no Ether) | Primordial (no Soul, no Ether) |
| What the partner borrows | Vessel's Soul and Ether | Host's Ether only |
| What the host gets | The Anomaly's distortion, personal to each pair | A fragment of one pole |
| Who picks the power | Set by the Anomaly's own contradiction | The Primordial; the host never chooses |
| Partner's influence | Can grow aggressive and try to take over | Cannot take control, wants the host alive |
| Effect on the mind | Psychological bleed, constant | Emotional simplification, only under strain or power use, per pole |
| Upkeep | Must be fed Remnants | Permanent drain on Ether reserves |
| Effect on Order powers | Blocks Transformation | Removes the element (bending only) |
| Worst outcome | Abyssal | Etherless, only if the Primordial is destroyed |
| Way out | Starvation, or a Chaos Contract | Destroy the Primordial |
| If the host dies | The bond ends | The pole returns to the Primordial, which loses its Ether supply |
| Limit | Not stated | A Primordial has at most two, one per pole |

Both Pact-holders and Contract-holders are known in-world as having a "pact"; **Pact** is the Void term and **Contract** the Chaos term.

## Places

The world map shows two landmasses, sixteen nations (Sek'luz and Mughierel share one footprint, one above and one below) and two polar countries.

| Area | Nations | Known so far |
| --- | --- | --- |
| West landmass | Farnnade, Xobbote, Borun, Hybrao, Arzennera, Zhoter, Naren, Sek'luz (above), Mughierel (below) | Xobbote: volcanic nation of smiths, between Farnnade and Borun. Zhoter: where Zayn grew up, with many Anomaly incidents. Hybrao: frontier walls and border conflict. Mughierel: underground country of Darkness benders, site of the experiments. Sek'luz: Light bender country on the surface above it. |
| East landmass | Mejennia, Balpo, Coerie, Jadea Pório, Jannia, Oajo, Neo Brard | Undeveloped |
| Poles | Romn (south), Tingsar (north) | Countries with different governments |

**Shape.** Places nest: world, landmass, nation, region, state, avenue. Trovic is the developed setting (the Guild is there; a state in Zhoter's North). Every place needs only a name and its parent; each has a development level (stub, partial, developed) so unfinished places are easy to list. Regions like "North" and "South" in character infoboxes are derived from the place tree. Optional sections: crystal resources, Chaos creature habitats, Dead or Distortion Zones, Liminal Zones, groups based there, and immigration. Each nation has an optional official bending, shown by the map color: the element of the majority, which comes from the founders. Long ago benders were segregated and each element founded its own nations; today they mix, so other benders live in every nation. A character's element and eye color never depend on the nation. Official bendings: Farnnade Earth, Xobbote Lava, Borun Fire, Naren Water, Balpo Air, Oajo Plant, Neo Brard Electricity, Sek'luz Light, Mughierel Darkness, and Romn and Tingsar (the poles) Ice. Gray nations (Hybrao, Arzennera, Zhoter, Mejennia, Jannia, Coerie, Jadea Pório) have none. Regions are compass subdivisions of a nation, such as the south of Xobbote or the north of Hybrao. Immigration is set per nation: Hybrao is very closed, Zhoter very open. Two places can share a footprint on different layers, above and below ground.

**Zhoter** is the first nation mapped in detail. It has ten states in four compass regions, each region with its own aesthetic.

| Region | Aesthetic | States and identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North (red) | Soft steampunk: steam, iron and discipline, a warm mechanical glow against a cold, pale landscape | Riquehen: industrial metropolis, "the engine of Zhoter". Kubetz: refining and heavy manufacture, "City of Cold Embers". Trovic: small fortified community on the border, last city before the Forest of Yaga |
| East (blue) | Maritime noir: humid, foggy, wet streets, secrecy under the trade | Durqosa: rich cosmopolitan port metropolis and home of the most popular bending fights, "the Blue Heart of Zhoter". Lupao: artistic, immigrant, "the Open Door" |
| West (green) | Gauchopunk: wide plains, wind, discipline and military readiness | Avernist: military and doctrinal metropolis, "the Western Blade". Driogoir: border logistics and customs, "the Dark Gates" |
| South (purple) | Naturalistic: lush, humid, in harmony with nature, the most spiritual and magical region | Telis: rich agribusiness center, very unequal. Solnos: working-class rural city, "the People's City". Gatusou: agricultural distribution and old traditions, "the Dark Granary" |

Under a nation the tree is region, then state; states contain no cities, only avenues. Trovic is one big state with no cities inside it, a single connected community divided only by avenues. The Guild and the main events are at the far north of Trovic. Each region is a small country of its own, with an *aesthetic* section (architecture, clothing, food, customs) and its own laws.

**Zhoter's founders** were friends who each ruled a piece and chose to stay one country: Jorge Alberto (North, Fire), Riardon (West, Fire), Icoeer (South, Air) and Linjack Seraphim (East, Light). The region colors on the map are the founders' own colors, not their elements. Zhoter has one president and its capital is Riquehen; each state has its own governor and no capital of its own. Regions are mostly independent. The founders get a short History section on Zhoter for now and their own pages later. Author-only: they are four characters from a tabletop RPG campaign, presented in-world as great historical figures.

**Landmarks.** Beyond Trovic lies the Forest of Yaga: dense, cold, foggy conifers, sacred or perhaps cursed. Cutting its trees is taboo and entering is discouraged. Past it, the Sek mountains form Zhoter's northern border, and beyond them lie Sek'luz and Mughierel, barely known, with no trade and almost no crossings. Reports say dangerous creatures come down from the mountains to Trovic. **Balance.** The north produces, the east connects, the west protects, the south preserves. A deliberate mystery sits at the edge, past Trovic, that no one in Zhoter has answered.

## In-world knowledge

The wiki holds what is *true*; characters do not know all of it. Each entry carries a **visibility** (public, scholars only, author only) and an optional note on who believes what.

- **Etherless:** real, never happened, nobody knows it is possible. No law exists for it.
- **Contract overrides Pact:** a possible escape route; whether anyone knows is undecided.
- **Non-Bender origin:** a minority of scholars think the cause is similarity, not opposition. Others call it a curse.
- **Primordial nature:** "a localized expression" and "the failure of Space and Time to remain separate" stay as competing interpretations, left to imagination on purpose.
- **Anomaly Remnants:** their true nature (anchor, residue, or more) is still debated.

**Knowledge tiers** (the cult): average members know the stated goal of coexistence; a handful know the Gardener Project; Kate knows fragments; only the Herald has the full picture. A cult member's pact status is *holds*, *none (waiting)* or *feigned*, and the feigned one is author-only.

## Build plan

**Stack:** Nuxt (same major version as work), Postgres in Docker, Drizzle (or the work ORM), Markdown for long text. **Pace:** about two hours a day, more on weekends. Only the author uses it at first, so there are no accounts; an *author mode* switch shows author-only content, and real login comes later.

**Data model:** entries (section, type, slug, name, parent, canon status, visibility, development level, redirect, JSON fields), sections (heading, Markdown, order, canon status, visibility), links (from, to, kind, role, note, current or former), aliases (with a retired flag). Section and type definitions live in a config file. Derived values (eye color, crystal, region, pole side effect, condition) are computed, and rule checks run on the server.

**Before starting:** Git repo with README and `.env.example`; old notes archived in `archive/` as retired material; seed files for 10 to 15 real entries (Zayn, Heleno, Sokah, Yel, Zhoter, Trovic, the Guild, Ether, Lava, the Void Pact, a Spectre, a crystal) with a script that rebuilds the database from them; Node LTS, Docker, a database GUI.

| Milestone | Result | Rough time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Setup | Nuxt and Postgres run, seed loads | 1 week |
| 2. First page | Zayn renders from the database | 1 week |
| 3. Sections | Six sections with index and entry pages | 1 to 2 weeks |
| 4. Links | Backlinks, aliases, redirects, search | 2 weeks |
| 5. Visibility | Author-only content never reaches the browser; canon status | 1 week |
| 6. Forms | Create, edit, delete | 2 weeks |
| 7. Rules | Derived values and rule checks | 2 weeks |

The times are rough guesses for a learning pace, about 10 to 12 weeks to a working version one. **Rhythm:** a weekday session is one small task; a weekend session is a milestone step plus a review and a backup.

**Not now:** clickable map, images, accounts and hosting, importing the old notes, alchemy details, the six missing side effects. New ideas go on this list first. **Watch for:** over-modeling, spoiler leaks, lost data (back up from week one), and content migration taking longer than the code.
