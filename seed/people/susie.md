Elias no longer exists. Susie is what survived.

Susie Elizabeth is the silent shadow at the edge of the battlefield, a patient and precise hunter who relies on observation rather than strength or leadership. She watches, waits, and strikes when the moment is right. Beneath her cold efficiency, however, is someone who has spent most of her life searching for a place to belong. She does not fight for glory, ideals, or recognition. She fights for the people who chose her, the family she built, and the home she spent years believing she would never have.

## Personality

Susie is quiet, observant, and deeply protective.

Years of neglect taught her to pay attention to things other people miss: shifts in tone, changes in behavior, the subtle signs that someone is upset, lying, or preparing to leave. While this makes her an exceptional scout and rogue, it also leaves her constantly anticipating rejection and disappointment.

Outwardly, she projects confidence through sarcasm, dry humor, and calculated indifference. She enjoys teasing the people she trusts, stealing small objects simply to see how long it takes someone to notice, and acting far less affectionate than she actually is. Much of her personality is built around creating emotional distance before anyone else has the chance to.

The contradiction at the center of Susie's life is that she desperately wants closeness while instinctively fearing it.

She wants people to stay, but constantly prepares herself for them to leave. She wants to be understood, but rarely volunteers anything personal. She wants connection, yet often hides behind walls that make connection difficult. Most of the meaningful relationships in her life exist because someone was patient enough to keep reaching for her after she had already started pulling away.

As a result, Susie expresses affection indirectly. She remembers details people forget about themselves, notices when routines change, and often solves problems before anyone realizes they exist. She checks on people without announcing it, leaves help where it is needed without taking credit, and quietly positions herself between danger and the people she cares about. Much of her love is invisible by design. If someone notices what she is doing, she will usually pretend it was accidental.

Her loyalty is absolute once earned. Susie does not divide the world into good people and bad people, nor does she care much about status, reputation, or ideology. Instead, she divides it into those she considers hers and everyone else. The people she loves become her highest priority, and protecting them becomes second nature.

Despite the confidence she projects, anxiety remains a constant companion. Part of her still expects relationships to disappear, happiness to prove temporary, and belonging to come with conditions. Even after finding people who genuinely love her, she sometimes struggles to believe they will stay.

## Backstory

Susie grew up in a home defined by emotional neglect. Though her parents were physically present, they were emotionally distant, leaving her with the belief that affection had to be earned and that taking up space was something she needed permission to do.

Searching for belonging, she drifted toward people who valued her skills but never truly saw her. She became useful, dependable, and difficult to replace, hoping usefulness would eventually become affection, but it never did.

Everything changed when she met [[kai]] and [[leon]]. For the first time in her life, she encountered people who expected nothing from her beyond honesty. They accepted her chosen name without hesitation and welcomed her into their lives without demanding she prove her worth first. When she expected conditions, they offered trust. When she expected judgment, they offered patience. When she expected to be left behind, they stayed. Meeting them challenged nearly every belief Susie had about relationships. More than giving her a home, they gave her permission to exist.

The name Susie became a symbol of that new beginning: a rejection of the silence, shame, and erasure that defined her childhood, and a declaration that she was allowed to live as herself.

Years later, when she took in [[sokah]] and [[yel]], she found herself doing for others what Kai and Leon had once done for her. Not because she saw herself as a hero, but because she understood exactly what it felt like to be lost, unwanted, and waiting for someone to choose you.

Her masks represent the identities she learned to wear in order to survive. Her stealth reflects a lifetime spent observing before trusting, while her ice bending mirrors the emotional distance she built around herself: beautiful, controlled, and difficult to approach.

In contrast, the name Susie represents the opposite. It is not a defense or another mask created for survival, but a choice. It is the part of herself she chose to keep.

## Character Flaws

### Anxious

Susie constantly anticipates rejection, abandonment, or failure, even in situations where there is little reason to expect them. Years of neglect taught her that belonging is fragile and that affection can disappear without warning.

As a result, she overthinks social interactions, assumes responsibility for problems she did not cause, and struggles to trust that people genuinely want her around. When afraid of being hurt, she often retreats behind sarcasm, emotional distance, or hostility, sometimes pushing people away despite desperately wanting them to stay.

While this caution makes her perceptive and difficult to deceive, it can also trap her in cycles of self-doubt, isolation, and unnecessary worry.

Much of Susie's growth comes from learning that she no longer has to earn every place she occupies, and that the people who love her are choosing to stay.
