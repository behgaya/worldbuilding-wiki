Leon Fritz Wilder is a builder in every sense of the word.

As the heart of Team Kai, he creates the stability that allows others to thrive. Whether he is standing between a monster and his friends, repairing a broken chair, cooking dinner, or rebuilding part of the Guild, Leon approaches every challenge with the same quiet philosophy: if something matters, it is worth taking care of.

He does not seek glory, recognition, or greatness. What matters to Leon is far simpler. He wants people to be safe, comfortable, and able to live their lives without carrying every burden alone.

## Personality

Leon is warm, approachable, and almost impossible to dislike. He has the energy of a favorite uncle, the kind of person who remembers birthdays, tells terrible jokes with complete confidence, and somehow ends up helping everyone around him without ever making it feel like an obligation.

Unlike many hunters, Leon rarely talks about strength. He talks about people.

He wants to know how someone is doing. Whether they have eaten. Whether they are sleeping enough. Whether they need help carrying something, fixing something, or simply talking through a problem.

His kindness is practical. He expresses affection through actions rather than words, building things for people, teaching them skills, making meals, repairing damaged equipment, or creating spaces where others feel comfortable. To Leon, caring about someone means investing time and effort into making their life a little easier.

His humor serves a similar purpose. The jokes are often terrible, the puns even worse, but they are deliberate. Leon understands that fear spreads quickly, and laughter often stops it from taking root.

Beneath his cheerful personality is someone remarkably dependable. In moments of crisis, his easygoing nature disappears almost instantly. He becomes calm, focused, and immovable, the person others instinctively look toward when everything else is falling apart.

Despite his reliability, Leon is surprisingly humble. He rarely sees himself as exceptional and often struggles to understand the impact he has on others. While many people view him as a mentor, protector, or role model, Leon usually thinks of himself as someone simply doing what needs to be done.

## Backstory

Leon grew up in Lupao surrounded by builders. His father repaired boats, homes, and furniture, while his grandfather had been a monster hunter. From both of them, Leon learned that useful work matters, whether that means fixing a roof or protecting people from monsters.

Though he dreamed of becoming a hunter, Leon spent years helping his family and working as a carpenter, finding purpose in building things that would last. Over time, he convinced himself that was enough.

It was [[kai]], his younger sister, who encouraged him to finally pursue his dream. With her support, Leon entered Hunter School later than most students, bringing with him a craftsman's mindset.

To Leon, carpentry and hunting share the same purpose: creating safety. As the Guild's carpenter, he helped build not just its structures, but its sense of home. Every repaired wall, handmade bookshelf, and reinforced workshop reflects the belief that the things worth caring about are worth maintaining.

## Character Flaws

### Comfortable

Leon is deeply content with simple, familiar things. Once he finds a life, routine, or responsibility that feels meaningful, he becomes reluctant to disrupt it, even when change might be necessary for his own growth. He often convinces himself that what he has is "enough," postponing ambitions, opportunities, or difficult conversations because he would rather preserve stability than risk uncertainty.

This tendency makes him dependable and emotionally grounded, but it can also make him resistant to change, slow to pursue his own desires, and prone to settling for less than he truly wants. Leon is often willing to fight for other people's dreams long before he fights for his own.
