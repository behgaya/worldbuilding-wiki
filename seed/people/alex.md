Alexander Byron Doyle is the quiet intelligence at the heart of Team Vulcan, a scholar, investigator, and archivist whose greatest strength is not power, but understanding. While others confront the world directly, Alex studies it, patiently collecting details until patterns emerge and hidden truths reveal themselves.

To Alex, knowledge is not merely information. It is a way of caring. Every note in his grimoire, every anomaly report, every carefully recorded observation is an attempt to preserve something that might otherwise be forgotten. Whether he is documenting a rare Ether phenomenon or remembering a friend's habits, Alex approaches both with the same quiet dedication.

## Personality

Alex is thoughtful, observant, and deeply curious, often noticing details others overlook and remembering them long after everyone else has forgotten. Though naturally introverted, he is not detached; beneath his reserved exterior is a sentimental person who forms deep attachments to the people and ideas that matter to him.

His curiosity extends beyond academics. While fascinated by Ether theory, anomalies, and ancient mysteries, he is equally interested in understanding people, approaching emotions and relationships with the same careful attention he gives his research.

Around those he trusts, Alex's dry observations and subtle wit often emerge. Despite his intelligence, however, he rarely sees himself as exceptional, tending to doubt his own conclusions before questioning others.

## Backstory

Born with prosopagnosia, Alex grew up unable to recognize faces, forcing him to rely on voices, habits, posture, and eventually Ether itself to identify others. Over time, this necessity shaped his worldview, teaching him to value people for who they are rather than how they appear.

His friendship with [[zayn]] became one of the first constants in his life. The cape Zayn wore so Alex could recognize him became a symbol of loyalty and belonging, later shared by [[emilia]] as well.

As he grew older, Alex developed a fascination with Ether theory, anomalies, and the legend of the Eternal Flame, a mystery that sparked a lifelong search for answers. This pursuit eventually led him into monster hunting, where he could study phenomena no library could offer.

The grimoire chained to his belt reflects that journey. Filled with observations, theories, and personal records, it serves as both a research journal and a reminder of everything he has learned, and everything he still hopes to understand.

## Character Flaws

### Timid

Alex is naturally hesitant in social situations, particularly around strangers, authority figures, or confrontation. He prefers observation to participation, often keeping opinions to himself until he is certain they are worth sharing. While this caution helps him avoid rash judgments, it can also make him appear distant, indecisive, or less confident than he truly is.

When conflicts become personal rather than intellectual, Alex often withdraws rather than pushing back, allowing stronger personalities to dominate conversations. He is far more comfortable questioning ideas than challenging people directly.
