The great metropolis of [[zhoter-west|the West]] and the military and doctrinal center of [[zhoter]]. Avernist is a fortified city of academies for soldiers and benders, with orderly streets and a constant military presence. It produces siege equipment, armor and tactical technology, and stands among the rolling grasslands of the West, swept by cold wind.

The city holds strong political influence, and many of the country's rulers come from it. Its people are proud, nationalistic and rigid. Avernist is the Western Blade, both shield and sword of the country.
