Zhoter is a nation of four regions and ten states, governed from its capital, [[riquehen]]. It borders the Sek mountains to the north, and it is open to the world: more than most nations, Zhoter takes in anyone who arrives.

## Geography

Zhoter is divided into four regions named for the compass: [[zhoter-north|the North]], [[zhoter-east|the East]], [[zhoter-west|the West]] and [[zhoter-south|the South]]. Together they hold ten states, each with its own character and its own governor.

The Sek mountains close off the northern border. Beyond them lie Sek'luz and Mughierel, two lands that are barely known in Zhoter and do not trade with it. Just past [[trovic]], the last city before the border, stands the Forest of Yaga, a stretch of sacred and cursed conifers where cutting a tree is taboo. Dangerous creatures come down from the mountains toward it, which is why Trovic is a fortified community.

## Government

Zhoter has a single president, seated in [[riquehen]]. Each state has its own governor, and the regions are cultural and historical divisions rather than political ones.

Zhoter has no official bending. Its four founders brought different elements, and over the generations they mixed so thoroughly that no single element defines the nation.

## Culture

Each region has its own colors and its own style, taken from the founder who shaped it:

- **The North** is red and industrial, a steampunk land of forges and engines.
- **The East** is blue, a maritime region with the feel of a port city at night.
- **The West** is green and rugged, a frontier of open land and military tradition.
- **The South** is purple and close to nature, a land of farms and old customs.

The regions depend on each other. The North produces, the East connects, the West protects and the South preserves.

## Founders

Four founders, one for each region, are remembered in Zhoter's history. Jorge Alberto founded the North and Riardon the West, both Fire benders. Icoeer founded the South, an Air bender, and Linjack Seraphim the East, a Light bender. Each region still carries its founder's colors.
