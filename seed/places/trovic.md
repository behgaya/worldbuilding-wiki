A state in the [[zhoter-north|North]] of [[zhoter]], on the border with the Sek mountains and the Forest of Yaga. Trovic is the city closest to the forest, and reports of dangerous creatures coming down from the mountains are constant, so it lives on permanent alert behind fortifications of stone and metal. Icy winds blow down from the Sek.

Small and poorly placed, Trovic makes up for it with a strong sense of community: its people all know each other and help each other. Their culture is resilient, wary of outsiders, and proud. Trovic has the steam and machinery of the North without the towering scale of Riquehen or Kubetz: its workshops and lamps give off a warm glow against the cold, pale landscape.
