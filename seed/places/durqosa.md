The great metropolis of [[zhoter-east|the East]] and the commercial, maritime and diplomatic center of [[zhoter]]. Durqosa is the richest and most cosmopolitan city in the country, with giant ports that receive foreign ships every day and elegant, colorful architecture full of squares and markets.

It is also deeply unequal, with wealthy neighborhoods on the coast and poor ones inland. Durqosa is the epicenter of the most popular bending fights, and many young people move here in search of fame. It is the Blue Heart of Zhoter: pulsing, alive and chaotic.
