A state in the [[zhoter-east|East]] of [[zhoter]] and the country's point of entry for foreign goods and culture. Lupao is a strong mix of cultures, immigrants and travelers, with lively markets full of crafts and short sea routes to nearby countries.

It is an artistic city, famous for its music and coastal festivals, and its economy rests on light trade and tourism. Its temperament is informal, carefree, even rebellious. Lupao is the Open Door, where the country breathes in the outside world.
