A state in the [[zhoter-south|South]] of [[zhoter]], the most farm-focused state in the region, home to its wealthy and the administrative center of its agribusiness. Telis is clean and orderly, with elegant buildings amid vast fields of crops, and it is where decisions about harvests and tariffs are made.

The city is controlled by wealthy landowners, while the workers live far away in satellite villages with little infrastructure. Telis is the Golden Harvest: beautiful on the outside, unequal on the inside.
