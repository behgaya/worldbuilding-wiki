The East of [[zhoter]] is a coast of drenched cliffs and ports forever wrapped in fog, where the wind blows in from the sea heavy with salt. It is home to [[durqosa]] and [[lupao]]. Whole cities balance between iron and sea, with streets wet from constant rain and neon lamps reflected in puddles of steam. It is a land where the day never seems to fully break, and where any shadow can hide a secret.

## Landscape

The eastern ports are dense and vertical, built on metal platforms, iron bridges and old mansions adapted to steam technology. The cliffs hold entire neighborhoods carved into the rock, linked by steep stairways and pneumatic elevators.

The climate is damp and cold, and the sea often rises in violent waves that batter the coastal walls, a constant reminder of the power of Naren, the East's neighbor and unstable partner across the water.

## Economy

The East is Zhoter's diplomatic and maritime center and its point of contact with the outside world. Its docks teem with goods, contraband and information, and it is said that nothing enters or leaves the country by sea without passing through the East first. The region profits from sea trade, the fishing of etheric creatures and steam transit, but what really moves it is information: knowing what others don't, and using it to get ahead of rivals, other regions and foreign powers alike. In the East, a secret is worth more than cargo.

## Culture

Easterners are discreet, observant and used to living among fog and scheming. They dress in a practical but elegant way: waterproof overcoats, leather gloves and goggles against the steam. Eastern art mixes decay and invention, with gear-driven theaters, smoky cafés and music played on brass saxophones and mechanical percussion.

The fog does not fall on everyone equally. Durqosa's wealthy coastal districts keep their bright façades, squares and markets, and Lupao's music and coastal festivals cut through the haze. The noir East and the colorful one live side by side, often a single street apart.
