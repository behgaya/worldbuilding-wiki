A state in the [[zhoter-north|North]] of [[zhoter]], devoted to refining and heavy manufacturing. Kubetz turns raw ore into alloys and industrial parts, and its sky is often clouded by smoke from the furnaces. The streets are wide for cargo and smell of metal.

Its population is mostly working-class and unionized: tough, resilient and hardworking. Kubetz is an old rival of [[avernist]] in arms production.
