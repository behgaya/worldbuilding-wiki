A state in the [[zhoter-west|West]] of [[zhoter]], a border city of logistical and customs importance. Driogoir is the inspection post for trade between regions, and many caravans pass through it every day along sharp roads cut into the terrain for fast transport.

It is full of government officials, smugglers and merchants, and its culture is tough and direct, with no frills. The climate is cool and hazy, with cold wind blowing across the open grassland. Driogoir is the Dark Gates, where nothing comes in or goes out unseen.
