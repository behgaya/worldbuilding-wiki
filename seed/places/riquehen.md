The capital of [[zhoter]], seat of its president, and the great metropolis of [[zhoter-north|the North]]: its industrial, administrative and military center. Riquehen is a vertical city of factories, workshops and towers of dark glass framed in copper, a hub of engineering, metallurgy and technomagical weapons, and home to the large corporations that draw resources from the whole country. Its nights are always lit.

Its people are hardworking, rigid and disciplined, and the climate is cold, with constant snow in winter. The nearby Forest of Yaga, to the north, weighs heavily on the city's life. Riquehen is the engine of Zhoter, where the whole country's way of life is decided.
