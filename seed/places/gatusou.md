A state in the [[zhoter-south|South]] of [[zhoter]], dedicated to agricultural distribution and traditional rituals. Gatusou is a mix of storage warehouses and old villages, and its people keep beliefs tied to the land, spirits and harvests.

Transport routes cross the city carrying food to the rest of the country, but almost none of those resources return to the local population. Seen as isolated but spiritually rich, Gatusou is the Dark Granary, carrying the weight of the nation.
