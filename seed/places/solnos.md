A state in the [[zhoter-south|South]] of [[zhoter]], a rural city of small farmers. Unlike the large estates of [[telis]], its farms are small family plots worked by the people who live on them, in simple homes surrounded by forest, rivers and mist, and its people stay closely connected to nature. The city suffers from a lack of public investment.

Even so, Solnos has a strong sense of community within its poverty. The big landowners have little control here, and the people are more united, with many rural festivals, strong traditions and a culture of their own. Solnos is the People's City: humble, resilient and full of life.
